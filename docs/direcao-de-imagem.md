## Prompt utilizado

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: full-bleed hero image for a sophisticated dental prosthetics laboratory website. Create an exceptionally refined photorealistic 3D product visualization of a floating upper full-arch dental prosthesis, sculpted from luminous pearl ivory zirconia ceramic. Only the teeth and their minimal ivory support, absolutely no gums, no pink, no human, no jaw, no roots. The arch has anatomically realistic individual teeth, subtle incisal translucency, satin smooth enamel with soft specular edges, high precision. Three-quarter frontal view from slightly above, front incisors at lower right, arch curves away towards upper left. Whole arch visible and floating at a gentle diagonal, like an exquisite high-tech precision instrument. Composition: wide 1536x1024 landscape. Place the dental arch in the RIGHT two thirds of the image (x=45%-92%), centered vertically, large but with breathing room. The LEFT 40% must be entirely empty almost black negative space for website headline. Backdrop pure near-black charcoal #090c0b, extremely subtle desaturated forest green ambient reflection behind object, no texture, no particles, no grid. Dramatic studio softbox light from upper left, restrained soft green rim from right. Luxurious premium biotech and Apple product photography, realistic materials and exquisite art direction. No text, no logo, no watermark, no UI, no annotations. This is a conceptual illustration and not a clinical case.

O PNG original permanece no diretório padrão de imagens geradas do Codex. O JPEG usado no site foi convertido para reduzir a transferência, sem edição do conteúdo.
